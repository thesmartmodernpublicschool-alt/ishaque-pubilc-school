Admission Information

Admissions are open for Session 2026-27 from Playgroup to Class 10. We welcome students who seek quality education in a values-based environment.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age-appropriate for the class applying for
  • Previous school leaving certificate (if applicable)
  • Successful interview / assessment for higher classes

Required Documents

  • Birth certificate / Form-B
  • Previous school report card / SLC
  • 2 passport-size photographs
  • CNIC copy of parent/guardian
  • Medical fitness certificate
